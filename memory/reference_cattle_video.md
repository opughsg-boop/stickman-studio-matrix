# Reference video analysis: cattle pen with rain (uploaded 2026-11-14)

Source: user's uploaded reel (26c2d1f3…mp4). Analysis by frame contact sheet and EBU R128 loudness only.

## Technical
- 720x1280 (not our 1080x1920), 30 fps, H.264 video, AAC stereo 48 kHz.
- Duration 32.4 s. Integrated loudness -14.1 LUFS, LRA 3.5 LU (matches our -14 LUFS target).

## Visual style (what is actually on screen)
- **Not hyper-realistic.** Animals are low-poly, stylised cattle with flat, saturated brown and white colours. Fences are simple boxes and cylinders.
- Backdrop is a flat saturated green cyclorama, not a sky. The island is a cutaway block: green grass top, brown soil cross-section, grey gravel base.
- Props: concrete water trough, round blue water puddles, a drinking channel, a wire-mesh/rail fence, a single small tree.
- Motion: continuous camera with fast motion-blur sweeps, heavy rain particles (white streaks) for most of the video, low-poly cows in clip-based idle/walk.
- Camera cuts in the sheet look like hard-cut swaps between shots with blur, not smooth zooms.
- Dark, wet soil and rain make several frames dim.

## Implications for our look
- The reference is closer to our diorama style (cutaway island, real-size pen, camera moves) than to the sugar-cane hyper-realistic look. Its realism is in layout and motion, not in model detail.
- Keep: green-or-sky backdrop option, cutaway soil, rain particle option, motion blur on camera sweeps, a clear scene per beat.
- Do not copy: low-poly cows as the main subject. Our locked look wants real models at real sizes (cow 1.4 m).
